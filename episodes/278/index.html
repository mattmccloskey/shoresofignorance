About This Episode

Matt and Michael return from two very different deserts. Vegas and Big Bend. One all lights and noise, the other all silence and heat.

Ep 278: Two Deserts

Then things get interesting.

Matt shares what it meant to scatter his father's ashes in West Texas. Michael talks about the solitude of crowds. And somewhere around the forty minute mark, the conversation turns into something else entirely. What is AI actually for? What gets lost when we outsource struggle? Why do we need to be broken to become whole? And what happens to a culture that forgets the purpose of things?

They land on teleology, the study of purpose itself. A knife only makes sense if you know it's meant to cut. So what are we meant for? And what happens when we stop asking?
