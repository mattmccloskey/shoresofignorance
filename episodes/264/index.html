Is the U.S. a Christian Country?

Episode 264 · January 21, 2026 · 90 min

Episode available on

About This Episode

After Don Lemon interrupts a church service in Minnesota, he calls the church goers entitled white supremacists who think this is a christian country.

We explore what makes a country christian, and what alternatives might be look like if we were to wish to be something different.
