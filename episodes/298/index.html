About This Episode

Matt and Michael start with ACL weekend and the arrival of fall. But the conversation quickly turns to something deeper. What are our artists telling us?

Matt shares his observation from watching ACL clips on Instagram. The performers all seemed low energy, emaciated, and disengaged. No rock and roll. No guitars. Just DJs and singers buttoned up to the neck. It sparks a bigger question. If artists are the first to catch the zeitgeist, what zeitgeist are they catching right now?

They explore the line between artist and propagandist. When Ed Sheeran uninvited Macklemore for making political statements at a concert, people demanded Sheeran pick a side. When a performer at ACL held a sign reading castrate the Cornell 7, it raised the question of whether that is art or propaganda. Michael shares how he left an Eddie Vedder concert early because the politics drowned out the music.

The conversation spirals through the algorithm as a false god. You are not a person on Instagram. You are traffic. Performers are not playing to people anymore. They are playing to a sea of iPhones broadcasting to the algorithm. The result is a culture that celebrates everything and scrutinizes nothing, where the only sin is asking whether something is actually good.

They land on the hardest truth of all. Matt confesses that even when he asks himself what he wants, the answer is not good things. His own desires are broken. And the only answer he has found is not in himself but in the one who says, I am the way.

Cheers y'all 🍻
