Fact Checks

  • Scott Adams announced that he has prostate cancer which has metastasized into the bone, the same prognosis as Joe Biden, and that he will most likely be leaving this earth in the next six months

    Needs Context

    Adams did announce, on Real Coffee with Scott Adams on May 19, 2025, the day before this episode was recorded, that he had metastatic prostate cancer that had spread to his bones, the same cancer Biden had just disclosed. He said he expected to be 'checking out' or that his life expectancy was 'maybe this summer,' which from mid-May is closer to two or three months than six. 'Same prognosis' also overstates the comparison: Biden's office had described his cancer as aggressive but hormone-sensitive and manageable, while Adams said he would not get better.

  • A phrase Scott Adams uses is 'one screen, two movies,' meaning we are all watching one screen but seeing two different movies

    Verified

    That is a signature Adams formulation, often worded as 'two movies on one screen,' which he used for years to describe polarized political perception.

  • Jake Tapper just came out with a book called Original Sin, about the cover-up of Joe Biden's infirmity, and was interviewed by Megyn Kelly, who asked how she had seen Biden's decline and he had not

    Needs Context

    The book, published in May 2025, is by Jake Tapper and Alex Thompson, not Tapper alone. Megyn Kelly did interview both authors on May 20, 2025, the day before this recording, and she did press them on why conservative media had reported on Biden's decline while they had not. Matt already flags that he is not quoting Tapper directly.

  • In the Gospel of John, at the Last Supper, Jesus says one of them will betray him, Simon Peter asks who, and Jesus says it is the one to whom he will hand the bread after dipping it in the oil; he hands it to Judas and tells him to do it quickly, and the disciples think Judas is going out to deal with the money

    Needs Context

    John 13:21-30 is the right scene. Peter motions to the disciple Jesus loved, who is the one who asks, 'Lord, who is it?' Jesus dips the morsel and gives it to Judas, then says, 'What you are going to do, do quickly.' Some thought, because Judas had the moneybag, that Jesus was telling him to buy what they needed for the feast or to give to the poor. Matt has the shape of the story; 'Simon Peter' is only partly right, and 'oil' is not in the text.

  • Iain McGilchrist says the left brain grasps ideas the way a hand clenches an object

    Verified

    In The Master and His Emissary, McGilchrist ties the left hemisphere to the right hand's grasp, and to the way we speak of 'grasping' an idea. That is the image Matt is using, including the habit of talking with our hands.

  • In Where the Red Fern Grows, you catch a raccoon by cutting a hole about 3 inches in diameter in a log, nailing nails at angles into the hole, and putting food inside; the raccoon can get its hand in and grab the food, but once it makes a fist it cannot pull it out, and it will not let go

    Needs Context

    The trap is in chapter 7 of Wilson Rawls's novel, and the fist-that-will-not-open is exactly the point. The details are off: Grandpa tells Billy to bore a hole about six inches across, drive horseshoe nails inward, and put shiny tin, not food, at the bottom. The raccoon reaches in, closes its paw around the tin, and is caught because it will not let go.

  • In The Brothers Karamazov, Ivan says that if there is no God, man should be permitted anything, that everything becomes permissible without a judge

    Verified

    That is the standard English rendering of Ivan Karamazov's argument, often shortened to 'if there is no God, everything is permitted.' Matt's wording is a fair paraphrase of that thesis.

  • Kamala Harris's campaign slogan was 'joy'

    Needs Context

    After Biden left the 2024 race, 'joy' was a major Harris campaign theme, especially around the convention, but it was not the official slogan. The campaign's main taglines were closer to 'A New Way Forward' and 'We're not going back.'

  • There was a banner above Auschwitz that said, 'Work will set you free'

    Verified

    The German slogan Arbeit macht frei was over the gate at Auschwitz and at other Nazi camps. Matt's English rendering is the usual one.

  • Václav Havel, in The Power of the Powerless, describes the big lie of a totalitarian state as everyone lying about everything all the time, including the cascade 'We know they're lying. They know they're lying. They know we know they're lying'

    Needs Context

    Havel's essay does describe a system so permeated with hypocrisy that it 'must falsify everything,' and people who do not believe the mystifications but must live within the lie. The cascading 'we know they are lying' formulation is a widely circulated Eastern Bloc saying, often attributed to Solzhenitsyn or left unattributed, not a passage from Havel.

  • It is better that a thousand guilty men go free than one innocent man perish, and that was Jefferson

    Incorrect

    The classic common-law formulation is William Blackstone's, and it is ten, not a thousand: 'It is better that ten guilty persons escape than that one innocent suffer.' Benjamin Franklin used a version with a hundred. 'A thousand' shows up later as a popular intensification, sometimes associated with Maimonides. Jefferson is the wrong attribution, and the number is not Blackstone's.

  • Jordan Peterson, talking about abortion, said abortion is clearly immoral, that nobody doesn't think it's immoral, and that the question is whether it should be illegal, because not everything that's immoral should be illegal

    Needs Context

    Peterson has said abortion is 'clearly wrong,' and that whether it should be illegal is a separate and harder question, because not every immoral act should be a crime. 'Nobody doesn't think it's not immoral' overstates the consensus, and 'immoral' is slightly stronger than the 'wrong' he usually uses, but the distinction Matt is taking from him is real.

  • Douglas Murray, in a new book called Democracies and Death Cults, about the Israel-Hamas conflict, says the ideology behind Hamas is a death cult, that about 1,200 Jews were murdered on October 7, and that there is film of Hamas terrorists murdering women and children and then calling their parents to say, 'I've just killed five Jews. Get mom on the phone'

    Needs Context

    The book, published in 2025, is On Democracies and Death Cults. The roughly 1,200 killed on October 7 is the standard figure, and Murray does dwell on terrorists filming the attacks and on a widely reported call in which a Hamas attacker boasts to his father, then asks him to put his mother on, that he has killed Jews with his own hands. In the call Murray quotes, the number is ten, not five.

  • Dostoevsky said you must be very careful never to lie, especially to yourself, because that's the road to death

    Verified

    In The Brothers Karamazov, the elder Zosima tells Fyodor, 'Above all, do not lie to yourself.' He warns that a man who lies to himself can no longer distinguish the truth and ends in a kind of spiritual ruin. 'The road to death' is Matt's gloss, but the attribution and the sense are right.

  • In Ted Lasso there is a line that the truth will set you free, but first it'll piss you off

    Verified

    Dr. Sharon Fieldstone says it to Ted in season 2. The line is older than the show, and is often associated with Gloria Steinem, but Matt is right that it is in Ted Lasso.
