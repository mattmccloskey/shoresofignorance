Fact Checks

  • The Last Duel, with Matt Damon and Adam Driver, is about the last legal duel, in the UK, in the 1700s or 1800s, between two counts or dukes after one man's wife accused the other of rape, and the movie tells the story from three points of view

    Needs Context

    The film and the three-perspective structure are right, and it is based on a real judicial duel after a rape accusation. The setting is not the UK in the 1700s or 1800s: it is France in 1386, the last judicial duel sanctioned by the Parlement of Paris, between the knight Jean de Carrouges and Jacques Le Gris, over the accusation made by Marguerite de Carrouges. Matt is openly unsure about the place and the century.

  • Twelver Shiism follows 12 descendants after Muhammad, the 12th went into hiding around the year 890, and about 90% of Iran follows this thread, especially the clergy and the ayatollah

    Verified

    Twelver Shiism is organized around twelve Imams in the line of Ali, and the twelfth, Muhammad al-Mahdi, is believed to have entered occultation rather than to have died. The usual dates are 874 for the Minor Occultation and 941 for the Major Occultation, so '890 or something' is in the right neighborhood. Iran is roughly 90 to 95 percent Shia, overwhelmingly Twelver, and that is the official doctrine of the clerical state. Michael already flags that he is not going to do this perfectly.

  • In Twelver Islam, the 12th Imam, a Christ-like figure, can come back only if Israel is wiped out, and that is a big stipulation in the religion

    Needs Context

    Classical Twelver eschatology does not make the destruction of the modern state of Israel a formal condition for the Mahdi's return. He is expected to reappear by God's timing, after a period of strife, and to establish justice. Some modern Iranian and IRGC interpretations do treat Israel as a barrier to his appearance, or say it will fall before he comes, but that is a political-religious reading, not a standard stipulation of the tradition Michael is trying to summarize.

  • On every measurable dimension, the COVID vaccines didn't help; they don't stop transmission and don't stop you from getting it

    Incorrect

    By the time of this June 2025 recording, it was well established that the vaccines did not fully prevent infection or transmission, especially after Omicron, and that protection waned. That is the part Michael has right. Matt's broader claim, that they did not help on every measurable dimension, is not. The shots substantially reduced severe disease and death, particularly in the earlier waves, which is a measurable dimension.

  • About 90 to 10 percent of a story is all you ever have, and you will not be 100% correct

    Needs Context

    Michael is describing an attitude toward evidence, not citing a study. The point is fair as a statement of humility; it is not a statistic.

  • Joe Biden said that if you are a Trump voter, you are garbage

    Needs Context

    In late October 2024 Biden did say, 'the only garbage I see floating out there is his supporters,' after a comedian at a Trump rally called Puerto Rico a floating island of garbage. The White House said he was referring to the insult, not to all Trump voters, and the line was messy enough that Matt's shorthand needs that context.

  • The number of illegal immigrants in the country is something like 50 to 70 million, including 20 million who came in under Joe Biden, and there are currently 7 million legal immigrants waiting to get citizenship

    Incorrect

    Matt is careful to say he heard this on the All-In Podcast and that nobody there disputed it, but the figures are far above standard estimates. As of this June 2025 recording, Pew's then-latest detailed estimate, from July 2024, put the unauthorized population at about 11 million in 2022, and other standard shops were in a similar range even after later border years. That is not a base of 50 to 70 million, and it is not 20 million new arrivals under Biden. There is a large legal-immigration backlog, often in the millions if you fold in family and employment visas, but '7 million waiting to get their citizenship' conflates that backlog with naturalization.

  • The population of California declined precipitously through COVID because of the way the government there was handling the situation, including free Medicaid for immigrants

    Needs Context

    California did lose population in 2020 through 2022, a historic change of direction, and by 2024 it had expanded Medi-Cal to adults without legal status, so both pieces have a basis. 'Precipitously' overstates the scale of the loss, and the decline had several causes, including housing costs and remote work, not only COVID policy or the later Medi-Cal expansion.

  • Arnold Schwarzenegger was a millionaire before he became a movie star, has never once auditioned for a movie because every part has been written for him, married a Kennedy, and became governor of California

    Needs Context

    The later résumé is right: he married Maria Shriver, of the Kennedy family, and was governor of California. He has long said that bricklaying, a mail-order business, and real estate made him rich before the blockbuster years. 'Never once auditioned' and 'every part has been written for him' are the oversell; he has told that story about later roles, but his early career, including films like Hercules in New York, went through ordinary casting.

  • Tim Ferriss, the 4-Hour Workweek guy, interviewed Arnold Schwarzenegger years ago

    Verified

    Ferriss did interview Schwarzenegger on The Tim Ferriss Show in 2015, a long conversation that is still posted on Ferriss's site. Matt is pointing to a real interview.
