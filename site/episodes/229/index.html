Fact Checks

  • The left in the 70s, through the ACLU, fought for the right of the KKK to have free speech, or the right of Nazis to march in downtown New York

    Needs Context

    The landmark fight is the ACLU's 1977-78 defense of the National Socialist Party of America's right to march in Skokie, Illinois; the group later held a rally in Chicago, not downtown New York. The ACLU also defended KKK speech in other First Amendment cases. Matt is pointing at that tradition, and he has the city wrong.

  • The Tuskegee experiment was a horrific case of racism in science

    Verified

    The U.S. Public Health Service's Tuskegee Syphilis Study (1932-1972) withheld treatment from Black men with syphilis and became a central example of racist abuse in American medical research.

  • RFK says 60% of our youth are obese, and 1 in 36 have autism

    Incorrect

    The autism figure is right as of this February 2025 recording: the CDC's then-latest ADDM number, published in 2023 from 2020 data, was 1 in 36 eight-year-olds. The youth-obesity claim is not. CDC NHANES data for August 2021-August 2023 put obesity among ages 2-19 at about 21%, not 60%. Adult obesity is near 40%, and overweight-plus-obesity among adults is much higher; Matt is recalling RFK's public-health riff and has the youth number too high.

  • RFK and Vance and Gabbard and Vivek defected from their party and joined Trump

    Needs Context

    RFK Jr. and Tulsi Gabbard had been Democrats and did leave that world for Trump. JD Vance and Vivek Ramaswamy were Republicans. Vance was a never-Trump critic who later became the vice-presidential nominee, which is a conversion inside the GOP, not a defection from the other party.

  • Kamala Harris reversed on every policy she ever held, and they were giving out free abortions and vasectomies at the Democratic National Convention

    Needs Context

    Harris did walk back several of her 2019 positions during the 2024 campaign, including on fracking, immigration, and a single-payer health plan, but 'every policy she ever held' is too clean. Planned Parenthood Great Rivers did run a mobile clinic in Chicago during the convention week that offered free medication abortions and vasectomies; it was about a mile from the United Center, and Planned Parenthood and fact-checkers were clear it was not sponsored by, or located at, the DNC itself. Matt even adds 'it literally had trucks outside.'

  • Liz and Dick Cheney endorsed Kamala Harris, and Democrats cheered that endorsement

    Verified

    In September 2024, both former Rep. Liz Cheney and former Vice President Dick Cheney said they would vote for Harris. Harris and other Democrats publicly welcomed the endorsements, which is the scene Matt is describing.

  • As Thomas Sowell says, there are no solutions, there are only trade-offs

    Verified

    Sowell writes in The Vision of the Anointed (1995), 'There are no solutions; there are only trade-offs.' Matt's wording is faithful.

  • The U.S. has $36 trillion in problems, or debt, added up

    Verified

    As of mid-February 2025, when this episode was recorded, total public debt outstanding was about $36.2 trillion.

  • Canada has a MAID program in which people who are suffering can be euthanized with their consent

    Verified

    Medical assistance in dying has been legal in Canada since 2016, and the eligibility criteria had already been expanded by the time of this recording.
