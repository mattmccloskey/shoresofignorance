About This Episode

300 shores visited. Our starting point is still ignorance. We don't even know what we're ignorant of. But we have a feel for how to get to the shore.

Eight years in, has all this talking made us braver? We talk about playing with ideas, not battling them. We also get into being misunderstood, curiosity versus dogma, and dying to yourself like a caterpillar in the goo.

Also, by the numbers we can see, 2026 is our best year yet. Thank you for listening.

Fact Checks

  • We started the show in 2018

    Verified

    The earliest episode in the feed (Ep 7, with David Ramirez) is dated Dec 15, 2018. Matt is right that it's the ninth year.

  • Ep 100 was "over two years ago"

    Needs Context

    True, but understated. Ep 100, "One Hundred," was published Jan 3, 2022, nearly five years ago.

  • Peterson: writing is how you learn to think

    Verified

    Peterson: "If you can think, and speak, and write, you are absolutely deadly. Writing is thinking formalized. You gain the ability to think by first learning to write very, very carefully."

  • Bret Weinstein moderated the Peterson vs Harris events

    Verified

    He moderated both Vancouver nights in June 2018. Douglas Murray joined for Dublin and London in July, acting more as a guide than a formal moderator.

  • Elon Musk's "Nazi salute"

    Needs Context

    On Jan 20, 2025, at the post-inauguration rally at Capital One Arena, Musk made two straight-arm gestures after saying "My heart goes out to you." The ADL called it an awkward, enthusiastic gesture; many historians and commentators called it a fascist salute. Musk dismissed the criticism. The gesture happened; its meaning is disputed.

  • Democratic socialists: "democracy at the ballot box and beyond"

    Needs Context

    Matt couldn't find it either. The closest common slogan is "democracy at the ballot box and in the workplace," used by DSA chapters and labor groups.

  • The Tesla Roadster flew and flipped at its unveiling

    Verified

    At the Oct 15, 2026 event at SpaceX's McGregor test site, the Roadster used a fan suction system and SpaceX cold-gas thrusters to launch over a lineup of Teslas, roll in midair and land. Production is planned for 2028.

  • AOC and Omar called Musk unintelligent

    Verified

    AOC (Feb 2025) called Musk one of the most unintelligent billionaires she'd met; Ilhan Omar (Mar 2025) called him one of the "dumbest, luckiest" people on Earth. Clips were remixed with the Roadster footage. A Newsom quote of that kind was not found.

  • Heather Heying publishes anonymous Covid Era Stories

    Verified

    Natural Selections' "Covid Era Stories" series began in early 2026; authors may use pseudonyms, and several pieces ran as "Anonymous." Matt wasn't sure of the name; it's Natural Selections.

  • The serpent promised open eyes, and Satan offered Jesus everything

    Verified

    Genesis 3:5: "your eyes will be opened, and you will be like God." Matthew 4:9: "All these I will give you, if you will fall down and worship me."

  • Hairs numbered, tears kept in a bottle

    Verified

    Luke 12:7 / Matthew 10:30, "the very hairs of your head are all numbered." Psalm 56:8, "put my tears in your bottle."

  • The average COVID death had six comorbidities

    Incorrect

    The CDC's Aug 2020 update said COVID-19 was the only cause listed for 6% of deaths, and the rest averaged 2.6 additional conditions or causes. The "6" is the 6% figure. Many listed conditions were complications of COVID itself, such as pneumonia and respiratory failure.

  • Median age of COVID death above average life expectancy

    Needs Context

    Same as Ep 299: true in the UK and several European countries, roughly at or slightly below life expectancy in the US. The skew toward the elderly is real.

  • Gad Saad's "suicidal empathy"

    Verified

    Saad's term for misdirected empathy that harms the one extending it. His book Suicidal Empathy: Dying to Be Kind came out May 12, 2026.

  • "I no longer live, but Christ lives in me"

    Verified

    Galatians 2:20. The seed image Matt looks up is John 12:24, "unless a grain of wheat falls into the earth and dies, it remains alone; but if it dies, it bears much fruit." Jesus' call to lose your life is Luke 9:23-24.

  • Caterpillars turn to goo in the cocoon

    Needs Context

    Mostly. Most larval tissue breaks down, but organized clusters called imaginal discs, plus parts of the nervous system, gut and tracheae, survive and build the adult. Butterfly-wing fragility is also overstated: touching rubs off scales but rarely ruins a butterfly's ability to fly.

  • "There is not one who is righteous. All are evil."

    Needs Context

    Romans 3:10-12 (quoting Psalm 14): "None is righteous, no, not one... no one does good." "All are evil" is a paraphrase, not the wording. Romans 3:23, "all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God," is exact.
