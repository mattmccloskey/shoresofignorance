About This Episode

Matt and Michael open by celebrating Medici's 20th anniversary. Michael reflects on two decades of coffee shops, community, heartbreak, and over a thousand baristas hired. The conversation turns to the cost of building anything meaningful. A business, a marriage, a family. And how knowing the cost upfront might stop you from ever starting.
