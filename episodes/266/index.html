Fact Checks

  • In the invisible gorilla study, the majority of viewers missed the gorilla

    Needs Context

    In Simons and Chabris (1999), about half of participants (roughly 46% overall) failed to notice the gorilla. The teams wore white and black shirts, not red and green, and viewers counted passes by one team.

  • The Carrington Event happened in 1859

    Verified

    The geomagnetic storm of September 1–2, 1859, followed a solar flare observed by Richard Carrington.

  • The Carrington Event knocked out the electric grid and radios kept working without power

    Needs Context

    There was no electric grid or radio in 1859. What failed was the telegraph network: operators reported shocks, sparks and paper fires, and some lines kept sending messages on auroral current alone with their batteries disconnected.

  • The sun throws off an 'SME — solar magnetic ejection'

    Incorrect

    The term is coronal mass ejection (CME), a burst of plasma and magnetic field from the sun's corona.

  • Low-latitude auroras are happening because Earth's magnetic field is weakening

    Needs Context

    Earth's dipole field has weakened by roughly 9% over the last two centuries, but the recent run of low-latitude auroras (for example the May 2024 G5 storm) is driven mainly by the peak of Solar Cycle 25, as of early 2026.

  • Breast milk changes by time of day and responds to a baby's saliva when the baby is sick

    Needs Context

    Milk composition does vary by time of day (for example cortisol and melatonin), and studies show immune factors rise when an infant is sick. The saliva 'backwash' mechanism is a leading hypothesis rather than an established fact.

  • Breast milk feeds the baby's gut microbiome

    Verified

    Human milk oligosaccharides are not digested by the infant; they feed beneficial gut bacteria such as Bifidobacterium infantis.

  • Our mitochondria have different DNA than we do

    Needs Context

    Mitochondria carry their own small genome (mtDNA) inherited from the mother, but it is still your DNA. Gut bacteria, by contrast, really are separate organisms.

  • Solzhenitsyn 'participated' in the Soviet system before writing about it

    Needs Context

    Solzhenitsyn served as a Red Army artillery officer and was then arrested in 1945 for letters criticizing Stalin, spending eight years in the Gulag. He wrote from inside the camps as a prisoner, not as a perpetrator.

  • The Gulag Archipelago helped bring down the Soviet camp system

    Needs Context

    The book (published in the West in 1973) badly damaged the USSR's moral standing. The mass Gulag had already been largely dismantled after Stalin's death in 1953, and the USSR itself fell in 1991.

  • Elon Musk said Tesla FSD figured out how to read signs without being taught

    Needs Context

    Musk has said the end-to-end neural network in FSD v12 learned behaviors from video rather than hand-written rules. Matt hedges on the specific claim, and we couldn't confirm a quote about reading.

  • Jurassic Park opens with a dinosaur footprint in the mud

    Needs Context

    Matt flags this as a 'for example'. The film actually opens with a raptor being unloaded at night; the famous early indexical moments are the rippling water cup and the excavated raptor fossil.

  • 'Avengers Ultimatum'

    Incorrect

    There is no film by that name. Matt likely means Avengers: Infinity War (2018) or Avengers: Endgame (2019).

  • 'Neither Jew nor Gentile' is a verse

    Verified

    Galatians 3:28. The 'barbarian, Scythian' wording Michael adds is from Colossians 3:11.

  • Paul tells us to treat elders as parents, peers as siblings

    Verified

    1 Timothy 5:1-2: older men as fathers, younger men as brothers, older women as mothers, younger women as sisters.

  • Illegally obtained evidence is inadmissible in court

    Verified

    The exclusionary rule, applied to the states by Mapp v. Ohio (1961), with exceptions such as good faith and inevitable discovery.

  • If you stare into the abyss, the abyss stares back

    Verified

    Nietzsche, Beyond Good and Evil, Aphorism 146 (1886).

  • 'Seek and you shall find; knock and the door will be opened'

    Verified

    Matthew 7:7 (also Luke 11:9).
