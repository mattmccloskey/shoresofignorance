About This Episode

Matt and Michael open with a meditation on digital trust in an age of voice cloning and AI impersonation. Then things get interesting. A Napoleon quote about Christ's empire of love launches them into a deep exploration of certainty, faith, and identity. They wrestle with the difference between certainty and certitude, the paradox of being certain of what we do not see, and why awareness itself can undermine confidence. The conversation builds toward a profound discussion of how Christ's reality transcends time and space, and what it means to be called not just to follow him, but to become him.

    Verified

    Verified

    Verified

    Verified

    Needs Context

  • Voice cloning technology requires only minutes of audio

    Verified

    Modern AI voice cloning requires only minutes of audio to create convincing replicas. Matt's claim about creating a voice ad from podcast audio three years ago is plausible given the technology available then.
