About This Episode

Michael opens with a question that has been rattling around his soul. What actually changes when someone goes from not knowing God to knowing God? Is it just semantics, or does something real happen? He traces the pattern through scripture. Israel crossing the Red Sea. Jacob wrestling God and becoming Israel. Simon becoming Peter. Saul becoming Paul. Every time, something shifts. A name changes. A person becomes someone new.

Matt tells the story of sneaking backstage at concerts as a broke college kid. No credentials, no lanyard. Just confidence. The hypothesis was simple. Act like you are supposed to be there and nobody stops you. It worked every time. This becomes a metaphor for what belief actually does. It is not just intellectual assent. It is a change in how you carry yourself in the world.

The conversation spirals through God's question to Adam in the garden. Where are you? Not a question about atoms and molecules. A question about presence. About where your heart is. They explore what it means to be maximally alive, why the just be a good person worldview collapses under the weight of real relationships, and why every human walking in circles without a landmark ends up right back where they started.

They land on fatherhood. Matt's two year old is learning that other people have names, but Dad is not a name. Dad is a category. The highest one. And maybe being born again is exactly that. Recognizing a Father you did not know you had.

Cheers y'all 🍻
