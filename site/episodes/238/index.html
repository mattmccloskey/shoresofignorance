Fact Checks

  • Consternated means filled with anxiety

    Needs Context

    'Consternation' is closer to sudden dismay, confusion, or alarm than to a general state of anxiety. Matt is feeling his way toward the word, and even asks what it means, so the definition he lands on is a usable approximation rather than the dictionary sense.

  • Carl Jung said, 'Neurosis is always the failure to engage in legitimate suffering'

    Needs Context

    The published description of this episode quotes the line correctly: 'Neurosis is always a substitute for legitimate suffering,' from Jung's Psychology and Religion. Matt has the attribution and the idea, but 'failure to engage in' is his wording, not Jung's.

  • In Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance, a man goes over to a friend's house, the faucet has been dripping for years, and the observation is that a thing ignored that long can end a marriage and you may not even know why

    Verified

    In Pirsig's book, John and Sylvia Sutherland's dripping faucet has gone unfixed, and Pirsig uses it as a sign of a deeper domestic strain, including resentment toward technology. 'For years' and 'ends a marriage' are slightly heightened, but the anecdote and the warning about unattended things are in the book.

  • The saying is, seek and you will find, knock, and the door will be open to you

    Verified

    That is Matthew 7:7, and Luke 11:9: 'Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you.' Michael's 'open' for 'opened' is only a slip of tense.

  • Winston Churchill said you need to make a good plan, but as soon as you start executing the plan, the first thing that goes is the plan

    Incorrect

    Matt is guessing, and the line is not a Churchill quotation. The usual sources are Helmuth von Moltke, 'no plan survives contact with the enemy,' and Eisenhower's 'plans are worthless, but planning is everything.' Michael then offers the closer match, which is Mike Tyson.

  • Mike Tyson said, have a plan, but after the first punch, the plan is out the window

    Verified

    Tyson's famous line is, 'Everybody has a plan until they get punched in the mouth.' Michael has the sense and the attribution right.

  • Peter Thiel says the thing that makes humans different from the rest of the animal kingdom is our ability to imitate

    Verified

    In their April 2025 conversation on The Jordan B. Peterson Podcast, the interview linked in this episode's published description, Thiel does discuss René Girard's claim that humans are distinguished by imitation, including the imitation of desire. That is the remark Matt is bringing into the show.

  • 'Imitation is the sincerest form of flattery'

    Verified

    The usual English proverb, often credited to Charles Caleb Colton. Matt uses it in passing, and the wording is right.

  • In the parable of the sower, some seed falls by the wayside, some onto rocky soil, and some into good soil, and the seed that falls into good soil pays back 60 to 90 fold

    Needs Context

    The parable is in Matthew 13, Mark 4, and Luke 8, and Matt has the soils right. The yield in Matthew and Mark is thirty, sixty, and a hundredfold, not '60 to 90.'
