About This Episode

The age of tolerance has given way to the primacy of the in group. Principles, friendship, even family, matter less than allegiances. This week we grapple with how our culture got here, and what we might do about it.
- The spiritual nature of division in the West
- You’re included in my circle, but I’m not included in yours
- The disturbing social division of politics
- How do we tolerate our neighbors in the age of tolerance
- If relationships aren’t foremost, what is?
- The hierarchy of beliefs and ideologies
- The end of the enlightenment
- The false god of science
- Evil is that which believes its knowledge is complete
- The need for a shared belief in a higher power to address simple ideas like, what is best and what is helpful for people
- Student loan forgiveness
