About This Episode

Matt and Michael survive the Texas Arctic blast and start with Davos. Trump's speech, Larry Fink's new WEF, Greenland, the cartels, and a race back to the moon. Is America as divided as our feeds say?

Then things get interesting. A Tony Robbins clip about self sabotage turns the mirror inward. Do we really want the truth? Or do we look away, from our own hearts and from the news? Matt and Michael pick apart the five year old ICE story, words that lost their meaning, and why AI can never replace a real thought filtered through relationship.

The back half goes deep. There is no right thing to say, only the true thing you risk saying. Pass the salt. Descartes and cover songs. Deadheads on pilgrimage. Chesterton's God who says do it again. And why we sometimes refuse the very thing that would make us happy.

Fact Checks

  • Only two presidents have spoken at Davos, and both were Trump

    Incorrect

    Bill Clinton addressed the WEF in person on Jan 29, 2000. Trump is the second sitting president to attend in person. He went in 2018, 2020 and 2026, and he spoke by video in 2025. Source: American Presidency Project, NYT.

  • Larry Fink is now chairman or heading up the WEF

    Verified

    In Aug 2025, BlackRock's Larry Fink and Roche's André Hoffmann became interim co-chairs of the WEF board after Klaus Schwab's exit. Source: WEF statement and Bloomberg.

  • The census counts bodies, not citizens

    Verified

    The 14th Amendment apportions House seats by "the whole number of persons in each state," and the census counts all residents regardless of citizenship.

  • By the 2030 census every blue state is predicted to lose seats and every red state to gain

    Needs Context

    The direction is right. Brennan Center projects that Harris-won states lose about 12 seats, and the American Redistricting Project says about 11. But not every blue state loses and not every red state gains. Mostly CA, NY, IL, MN, OR, PA, RI and WI lose, and mostly TX, FL, NC, AZ, UT and ID gain. Newer NDRC estimates show smaller shifts. The motive Matt relays for Democrats' immigration stance is one commentator's theory, not an established fact.

  • ICE and the 5-year-old in Minnesota

    Needs Context

    Liam Conejo Ramos was detained with his father in Columbia Heights on Jan 20, 2026. Matt's account matches DHS/ICE's version: the father fled, the mother refused to take the boy, and the father asked to keep him. School officials, neighbors and the mother dispute it. They say the father did not flee, the boy was led to the door as "bait," and the mother stayed inside out of fear of arrest. A federal judge later ordered both released. Sources: AP, ABC, MPR, PBS.

  • NASA doesn't have the ability to launch rockets anymore

    Incorrect

    NASA retired the Space Shuttle in 2011 and relies on SpaceX for ISS crew flights. But it has its own SLS rocket, which flew Artemis I in 2022 and launched the crewed Artemis II lunar flyby on April 1, 2026. Source: NASA.

  • Viral imagery around Alex Pretti was AI generated

    Verified

    Reuters, PolitiFact and Full Fact documented AI-altered images of the Jan 24, 2026 Minneapolis shooting, including ones adding a gun to his hand. The transcript spells the name "Preddy."

  • Descartes arrived at "I think therefore I am" after realizing most of his beliefs were taken on authority

    Verified

    In the Discourse on Method and the Meditations, Descartes resolves to doubt everything he had received from others and from the senses, and finds the cogito is the one certainty left.

  • Chesterton's "do it again

    Verified

    Orthodoxy, chapter 4, "The Ethics of Elfland": children say "Do it again," and perhaps God says "Do it again" to the sun and to every daisy. Matt said daffodils, but the text says daisies.

  • Peterson's "sit on the edge of your bed" exercise

    Verified

    Peterson often recommends sitting on your bed and asking what you're doing wrong that you could fix, warning that you will get an answer you don't want.

  • The Christopher Walken "pass the salt" acting quote

    Needs Context

    It is widely attributed to Walken, but we couldn't find a primary source for the exact wording.

  • Never Say Never" comes from Fievel Goes West

    Incorrect

    "Never Say Never" is sung by Henri, voiced by Christopher Plummer, in the original *An American Tail* (1986). Fievel Goes West (1991) features "Dreams to Dream."
