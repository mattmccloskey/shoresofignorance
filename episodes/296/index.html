About This Episode

Matt and Michael start with breathing exercises and end up in the deep end of theology. What begins as a conversation about Wim Hof and the symbolic power of breath becomes an exploration of the Logos. The divine Word that John says was in the beginning. Through which all things were made.

Matt shares the story of his divorce and the strange experience of watching two people's realities separate until communication becomes impossible. This leads to a fundamental question. If we can't agree on a shared reality, what happens? The answer, they argue, is violence. Unless there's a higher order, a Logos, to which both parties submit.

The conversation spirals through the nature of truth and lies. Why lying creates an anti-reality you must maintain. Why even atheists can't escape submitting to something. Michael makes the case that submission is the only true act of free will. Matt connects this to C.S. Lewis's The Four Loves and why friendship requires a third object that both people face together.

They land on the Christian claim that Jesus is the Logos made flesh. The ultimate reality who reveals himself, because we cannot build our way up to God. The Tower of Babel always fails. The Word must come down.
