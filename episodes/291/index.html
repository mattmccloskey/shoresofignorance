About This Episode

Matt and Michael open with the paradox of getting your ass kicked and somehow feeling more alive because of it. Matt shares his realization that there is no "there" to arrive at. The car ride IS the destination. Flat tires, closed restaurants, broken grinders. That's not the interruption of life. That IS life. Then things get deep. They wrestle with Dawkins vs. Lennox on justice and whether the universe has a moral order. They explore why science itself presupposes God, why atheism requires its own kind of faith, and why Lennox's answer about morality is devastating. All men are created in the image of God, even the ones who don't believe in Him. The back half dives into inversion. How we try to make reality conform to us instead of conforming ourselves to reality. They tackle sex, polyamory, pornography, and C.S. Lewis's insight from The Abolition of Man. If you optimize for the purpose of a thing, you destroy the thing itself. Optimize for pleasure, and you end up in misery. Cheers y'all 🍻

  • Picasso painted ~150,000 works

    Estimates vary widely (13,500 paintings to 150,000 total works including prints, ceramics, etc.). The 150K figure likely refers to total artworks, not just paintings. Matt said "150,000 paintings" — needs context.

  • Michael Jordan's field goal percentage

    ✅

    Career FG% was 49.7%, not 7%. The 7% figure likely refers to "success rate" in a broader sense (championships vs. seasons, iconic moments vs. total games). Needs context.

  • 1 in 10 small businesses survive

    ✅

    Commonly cited stat. BLS data shows ~20% fail in year 1, ~50% by year 5. The "1 in 10" is a rough approximation for long-term survival.

  • Wayne Gretzky "You miss 100% of the shots you don't take"

    ✅

    Widely attributed to Gretzky. Verified.

  • Margaret Thatcher "The problem with socialism is you eventually run out of other people's money"

    ✅

    Attributed to Thatcher. She said "socialism" not "communism" as Matt quoted. Minor misquote.

  • Married people have more sex

    ✅

    Multiple studies confirm married/cohabiting couples report higher sexual frequency than singles. Verified.

  • Children respond to seeing biological parents' love

    ✅
