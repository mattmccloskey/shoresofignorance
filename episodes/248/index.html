Fact Checks

  • Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce got engaged around the time of this recording

    Verified

    The couple announced the engagement on Instagram on August 26, 2025, the day before this episode was recorded. Matt's 'did you hear' is timely, not a rumor.

  • SpaceX's Starship flew a test the same day, deployed satellites, and precision-landed in the Indian Ocean next to a camera placed there ahead of time

    Verified

    Starship Flight 10 launched on August 26, 2025. It deployed eight dummy Starlink satellites, then did a landing burn and soft splashdown in the Indian Ocean beside a pre-positioned Starlink-equipped buoy camera. After splashdown the ship fell over and broke up, which is the 'it blew up' footage Michael is answering.

  • Google released an image model nicknamed Nano Banana around this time

    Verified

    Gemini 2.5 Flash Image, internally and popularly called Nano Banana, was rolled out on August 26, 2025. Matt has the name right.

  • No weapons of mass destruction were found in Iraq after the 2003 invasion

    Verified

    The principal WMD stockpiles used to justify the war were not found. Michael's memory of making 'the jump' from 9/11 to WMDs, then justifying it after the fact, matches a widely shared public arc.

  • Christoph Waltz said he did not play Hans Landa as an evil man who knows he is evil, but as someone who thinks he is good

    Verified

    In interviews after Inglourious Basterds, Waltz repeatedly refused the 'how did you get into an evil frame of mind' premise. He argued there is no such frame, and that a figure like Landa, or even Himmler, would not have understood the question. Michael's retelling is fair.

  • M. Scott Peck, in People of the Lie, recounts parents who gave their son the gun his brother had used to kill himself as a Christmas gift

    Verified

    In the book, 15-year-old Bobby's older brother Stuart had killed himself with a .22 rifle. At Christmas the parents gave Bobby that same rifle. Peck treats the parents' inability to see the cruelty of the gift as an example of ordinary evil. Peck was a psychiatrist; he also wrote The Road Less Traveled, and he died in 2005, which answers Matt's 'I don't know if he's still alive.'

  • Dostoevsky wrote, 'Above all, don't lie to yourself… and having no respect, he ceases to love'

    Verified

    The passage is from Father Zosima in The Brothers Karamazov (Book II). Matt's wording is a close popular rendering: many translations have 'comes to such a pass' where he says 'comes to a point,' and then the respect-and-love close is the same.

  • Solzhenitsyn wrote that the line between good and evil runs through every human heart

    Verified

    In The Gulag Archipelago he writes that the line dividing good and evil 'cuts through the heart of every human being.' Matt's 'right down the heart of every man' is the usual short form.

  • Jesus said the two greatest commandments are to love God and love your neighbor

    Verified

    Matthew 22:37–40 and Mark 12:29–31. Michael is right that they are framed as positive commands, not 'shall nots,' though they do not simply erase the Ten Commandments in Christian teaching.

  • The Bible says all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God

    Verified

    Romans 3:23, which Michael is paraphrasing when he talks about the confession that starts Christianity.

  • Paul wrote that we do not war against flesh and blood, but against powers and principalities

    Verified

    Ephesians 6:12: 'we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers over this present darkness.' Michael's wording is a standard paraphrase.

  • Michelangelo's Pietà is in the Vatican and shows Mary holding the dead body of Jesus

    Verified

    The sculpture is in St. Peter's Basilica. Michael and Matt have the image right. 'The good mother fails' is a saying Jordan Peterson uses and sometimes traces to psychoanalytic tradition, not a caption on the statue itself.

  • Carl Jung wrote, 'People don't have ideas. Ideas have people'

    Needs Context

    The line is widely attributed to Jung and fits things he did write about ideas and complexes taking possession of a person. The exact wording is a popular paraphrase, not a clearly sourced sentence in his collected works.

  • CDC and WHO redefined 'vaccine' and 'pandemic' so that COVID would fit

    Needs Context

    CDC did change its public wording for 'vaccine' and 'vaccination' in 2021, swapping 'immunity' for 'protection' or 'immune response,' and said it was to avoid implying 100% effectiveness. The claim that WHO rewrote 'pandemic' to include COVID usually points back to a contested 2009 H1N1-era wording debate, not a fresh 2020 redefinition. Matt's 'not a vaccine by the old definition' also folds in the move from classic live/killed shots to mRNA. There is a real definitional fight here, but it is messier than a simple late-game rename.

  • A Democratic memo was circulating in August 2025 telling the party which words, including cisgender, to stop using

    Verified

    In late August 2025 the centrist group Third Way published a memo urging Democrats to drop language such as 'cisgender,' 'birthing person,' and 'privilege' because it was alienating voters. Scott Jennings and others discussed it in that news cycle, which is what Michael is remembering.

  • The official report on the JFK assassination has a well-known name

    Verified

    Matt cannot recall it in the moment. It is the Warren Commission report (1964).

  • Barack Obama narrated a nature documentary that tells a moral story over animals killing and eating each other

    Verified

    Our Great National Parks, a 2022 Netflix series, is narrated by Obama. Matt has been watching it with his one-and-a-half-year-old; the pastoral voice-over over predation is a fair description.

  • Most U.S. humanitarian-aid and NGO money was being laundered into the pockets of politicians and private operators

    Needs Context

    By August 2025 there was a loud public fight over USAID and NGO waste, fraud, and political self-dealing, and some real cases had surfaced. 'The vast majority of that money was being laundered' overstates what had been shown. Matt is voicing a widely circulated charge, not an audited finding.

  • Worldwide climate policies, if implemented, would wipe out half of humanity

    Needs Context

    Matt is describing a view he hears in climate politics, and some degrowth or energy-restriction scenarios would be devastating for the global poor. The 'wipe out half of humanity' figure is a rhetorical ceiling, not a demonstrated outcome of the policies actually on the table.
