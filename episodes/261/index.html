Fact Checks

  • Matthew McConaughey's first words on screen were 'All right, all right, all right,' at Top Notch

    Verified

    McConaughey has said the line, improvised in a Top Notch scene in Dazed and Confused (1993), was the first thing he ever said on camera in a film.

  • Dazed and Confused was based on Huntsville High School

    Verified

    Richard Linklater graduated from Huntsville High School in Texas, and the film draws on his experiences there, though it is set in Austin in 1976.

  • Next Wednesday is Christmas Eve, and the Wednesday after is New Year's Eve

    Verified

    Christmas Eve 2025 and New Year's Eve 2025 both fell on Wednesdays.

  • The show started around August 2018 and went weekly in 2020

    Verified

    The first episodes were published to the feed on September 24, 2018, so recording in the late summer of 2018 fits, and the feed shows a weekly cadence from January 2020.

  • C.S. Lewis said you can't go back and change the beginning, but you can start where you are and change the ending

    Incorrect

    The line does not appear in Lewis's writings. Quote Investigator traces it to James R. Sherman's 1982 book Rejection, and later versions were credited to Carl Bard.

  • Viktor Frankl said the space between stimulus and response is your freedom

    Incorrect

    The Viktor Frankl Institute says the quote is not in Frankl's work. Stephen Covey popularized it after reading it in a book whose author he couldn't recall, and the closest known source is Rollo May.

  • 'What could you do that you would do' is a Jordan Peterson phrase

    Verified

    In 12 Rules for Life (2018), Rule 4, Peterson suggests asking, 'What could I do, that I would do, to make Life a little better?'

  • Carl Jung said every story has a villain, and you should make sure it isn't you

    Incorrect

    There is no such line in Jung's work. 'Every story has a villain' is a John Eldredge line, and the 'make sure it isn't you' version is a modern motivational paraphrase. Jung's related idea is the shadow, the dark side of ourselves we tend to project onto others.

  • Matt first heard Chamath Palihapitiya on the Lex Fridman Podcast around 2022

    Verified

    Chamath appeared on Lex Fridman Podcast #338, released November 15, 2022.

  • New legislation just passed that is hitting the THC beverage industry

    Verified

    The November 12, 2025 law ending the government shutdown redefined hemp to exclude products with more than 0.4 milligrams of total THC per container, effectively banning most hemp THC drinks one year later. A September 2026 funding bill later pushed that cap's start date to December 11, 2026.

  • Solzhenitsyn concluded that every person is individually responsible for everything that happens in the world

    Needs Context

    The idea is most directly Dostoevsky's, from Father Zosima in The Brothers Karamazov: each of us is responsible for everything and to everyone. Solzhenitsyn's Gulag Archipelago makes a related case, that the line between good and evil runs through every human heart, and Jordan Peterson often pairs the two.

  • H-E-B handled the start of COVID unusually well

    Verified

    H-E-B had kept a pandemic plan since the mid-2000s and began acting on it in January 2020, and its supply and rationing response was widely praised in Texas and national coverage.

  • Bryan Johnson wants to never die

    Verified

    Johnson's Blueprint regimen and 'Don't Die' project aim to slow and reverse aging, and he has said his goal is to not die.

  • Michael Dell and his wife just gave a large sum of money away

    Verified

    On December 2, 2025 Michael and Susan Dell pledged $6.25 billion to put $250 into Trump Accounts for up to 25 million children.

  • The Abolition of Man makes the case against transhumanism and gene editing

    Needs Context

    Lewis's 1943 lectures are mainly about education and objective values, which he calls the Tao. The third lecture warns that 'man's conquest of Nature' becomes the power of some men over others, including through eugenics and conditioning of future generations, which anticipates later debates over gene editing without addressing them directly.

  • The serpent told Adam and Eve they could be like God

    Verified

    Genesis 3:5: 'you will be like God, knowing good and evil.'

  • Scripture says the angels are jealous of man because we have will

    Needs Context

    No verse says this. 1 Peter 1:12 speaks of things 'into which angels long to look,' and classical Christian theology, including Aquinas, holds that angels do have free will, which is how some of them fell.

  • Steve Jobs said you hire people so they can tell you what to do

    Verified

    In a 1996 Fresh Air interview Jobs said, 'We hire people to tell us what to do.' The popular version, 'It doesn't make sense to hire smart people and then tell them what to do,' is a widely shared paraphrase.
