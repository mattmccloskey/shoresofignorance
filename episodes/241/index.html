Fact Checks

  • Chamath Palihapitiya says there is no difference between failure and learning, because you don't learn very much from success

    Needs Context

    Chamath has often talked about failing, iterating, and learning in public, and the sentiment is in his usual register, but this is not a single famous line the way Matt tells it, and 'no difference' overstates the point even if the lesson is fair.

  • In Hebrews, the spirit can divide bone and marrow

    Needs Context

    Hebrews 4:12 says it is the word of God, living and active, that pierces to the division of soul and spirit, of joints and of marrow. Michael is reaching for the right passage and the right image of discernment, but he attributes the dividing to 'the spirit' rather than to the word.

  • In The Dark Knight, Heath Ledger's Joker burns the pile of stolen money to show that wealth and prosperity do not matter to him, only destruction

    Verified

    That is the scene in Nolan's film: after the mob's money is stacked, the Joker sets fire to it, including his own share, to make exactly the point Matt is making.

  • Cain killed his brother and then went out and started wars

    Incorrect

    In Genesis 4, Cain kills Abel, is marked, and goes to the land of Nod, where he founds a city. The Bible does not say he started wars. The reading of resentment and the spirit of Cain is interpretive; the wars are not in the text.

  • Mayor Karen Bass said, 'We're standing by all Angelenos, whether they have paperwork or not'

    Needs Context

    On June 8, 2025, four days before this episode, Bass told a press conference, 'we stand with all Angelenos no matter where you were born.' That is the same stance Matt is hearing, but it is a paraphrase, not the sentence he quotes, and 'paperwork' is his wording, not hers.

  • 80% of the country is united on the immigration debate, on there being a difference between an immigrant and an illegal immigrant

    Needs Context

    Around this period, polls often found something like 80% support for deporting undocumented people convicted of violent crimes, and substantial but much lower support for a broader crackdown. The country was not 80% united on the definitional point Matt wants, and by late June a PBS/NPR/Marist poll also found a majority saying ICE had gone too far.

  • In the story of Sodom and Gomorrah, Abraham bargains God down from 100 righteous people to 1

    Incorrect

    In Genesis 18, Abraham starts at 50, then 45, 40, 30, 20, and stops at 10. God agrees each time. He never starts at 100, and he never lands on one. Matt is right that it is a negotiation, and he is visibly guessing at the numbers.

  • Solzhenitsyn's idea is that the line between good and evil is drawn down the heart of every man

    Verified

    The famous sentence in The Gulag Archipelago is that the line dividing good and evil cuts through the heart of every human being. Matt's wording is slightly looser, but the attribution and the sense are right.

  • In The Lord of the Rings, cypress trees try to eat Sam and Merry, and Tom Bombadil rebukes the tree until it recedes; he also comes in as a bear

    Incorrect

    Old Man Willow, not a cypress, traps Merry and Pippin in the Old Forest, and Bombadil does come singing and tells the tree to let them go. Sam is there, but he is not the one swallowed, and the name is Merry, not Mary. The bear is a different character: Beorn, from The Hobbit, which is the image Michael is reaching for when he says Bombadil 'came in as the bear.'

  • In The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe, Susan asks if Aslan is safe, and Tumnus says he is not safe, but he is good

    Needs Context

    Susan does ask, 'Is he—quite safe?' The answer, 'Who said anything about safe? 'Course he isn't safe. But he's good,' is spoken by Mr. Beaver, not by Tumnus. Matt already flags that he is not sure who says it.

  • Dostoevsky said that if man were left to eat cake, bathe in pools of water, and continue the species, the first thing he would do is break something so that something interesting would happen

    Verified

    That is a fair paraphrase of Notes from Underground: if you showered a man with every blessing and left him only to sleep, eat cakes, and continue the species, he would still play some loathsome trick, and it is sometimes very pleasant to smash things. 'Bathe in pools of water' is Matt's color; the sense of the passage is right.
