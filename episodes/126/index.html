About This Episode

Speaking truth amounts to nothing if that truth does not lead to action. In a time when the world is filled with an overwhelming variety of different ‘truths,’ it’s difficult to know who to believe, and even more obscure is knowing what action to take. Today, Matt and Michael discuss the power of speech to shape the world, for better or worse.
