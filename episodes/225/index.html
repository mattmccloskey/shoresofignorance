About This Episode

Elon salutes Hitler? The Left has become “the boy who cried wolf,” and Americans seem to be over it. The problem is that there’s a danger in that - we don’t want the boy who cried wolf to die all alone when the wolf actually comes. We consider possible solutions to this danger, and contend that the only solution is spiritual, not legislative.
