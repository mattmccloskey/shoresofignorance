Fact Checks

  • Minnesota is releasing foreign-born criminals so ICE can't detain them

    Needs Context

    As of February 2026, Hennepin County and Minneapolis policies limit honoring ICE detainers without a judicial warrant, so some arrested noncitizens are released on normal terms instead of being held for ICE. Officials describe this as declining to hold people past their release date, not releasing them in order to block ICE.

  • Rome always released a prisoner at Passover

    Needs Context

    All four Gospels describe the custom (Mark 15:6, Matthew 27:15, John 18:39). No source outside the Bible records it, and historians debate it.

  • Barabbas was a known murderer

    Verified

    Mark 15:7 and Luke 23:19 describe him as imprisoned for insurrection and murder.

  • John 1 says the world did not recognize him

    Verified

    John 1:10: 'the world was made through him, yet the world did not know him.'

  • Mattias Desmet came up with 'mass formation psychosis' around 2020

    Needs Context

    Desmet, a Ghent University psychologist, developed the idea of 'mass formation' in 2020–21 and in his 2022 book. The word 'psychosis' was popularized by Robert Malone on Joe Rogan's podcast in December 2021, and Desmet himself avoids it.

  • Philip Rieff (or Charles Taylor) described three cultures

    Verified

    It's Rieff, in My Life among the Deathworks (2006): first (pagan) and second (faith) cultures rest on a sacred order, while third culture denies any sacred order.

  • Far more people were deported under Obama

    Needs Context

    DHS counted about 3 million removals in FY2009–2016, versus about 1.5 million in Trump's first term. In Trump's second term, the administration reported around 600,000 removals in its first year, a much faster pace.

  • ICE shot 63 people under Obama and 23 of them died

    Needs Context

    No official count exists. The closest public figure is an investigation by The Trace and Business Insider that found 59 ICE shootings and 23 deaths from 2015–2021, a period spanning the Obama, Trump and Biden administrations.

  • Chesterton: 'Tolerance is the virtue of the man without convictions'

    Needs Context

    Widely attributed to Chesterton (often citing a 1920s column), but a primary source is hard to pin down.

  • Locke's original line was 'life, liberty and the pursuit of property'

    Needs Context

    Locke wrote of 'lives, liberties and estates' in the Second Treatise (1689), usually summarized as 'life, liberty and property'. Jefferson's 'pursuit of happiness' also echoes Locke's Essay Concerning Human Understanding.

  • Jefferson cut the miracles out of his Bible

    Verified

    The Life and Morals of Jesus of Nazareth (completed around 1820), known as the Jefferson Bible, removes the miracles and the resurrection.

  • God tells Moses 'I am that I am'

    Verified

    Exodus 3:14.

  • The serpent promises Eve she will be like God

    Verified

    Genesis 3:5: 'you will be like God, knowing good and evil.'

  • There is no evidence for learning styles; combining modes is what helps

    Verified

    Reviews such as Pashler et al. (2008) find no good evidence that matching teaching to a preferred 'style' improves learning, which is the point of Veritasium's 2021 video. Combining words and visuals (dual coding) does help most learners.

  • The movie with Billy Bob Thornton and Bruce Willis as cellmates

    Verified

    Bandits (2001), directed by Barry Levinson. Thornton plays the hypochondriac Terry Collins.
