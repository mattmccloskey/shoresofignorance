Fact Checks

  • OpenClaw is an open-source project started by Peter Steinberger

    Verified

    Steinberger (founder of PSPDFKit) created it; it was first known as Clawdbot and then Moltbot before being renamed OpenClaw in early 2026.

  • F-35s were costing about $120 million each when they were supposed to cost about $90 million

    Needs Context

    Early low-rate production F-35As cost well over $100 million each, and the program was famously over budget. Unit prices later fell to roughly $80 million for the F-35A (Lots 15–17).

  • ChatGPT came out in 2022

    Verified

    OpenAI released ChatGPT on November 30, 2022.

  • A man used Claude Code to control his DJI robot vacuum with a game controller and got access to about 7,000 of them

    Verified

    In February 2026 Sammy Azdoufal reverse-engineered his DJI Romo with Claude Code. DJI's servers let his token see about 7,000 devices in 24 countries, including camera feeds and floor maps. He reported it and DJI patched the flaw on February 8–10, 2026. Some reports say he used a PS5 controller rather than an Xbox one.

  • AI has long passed the Turing test

    Needs Context

    In a 2025 UC San Diego study, GPT-4.5 was judged human 73% of the time in a three-party Turing test. 'Long passed' is debatable, since that was the first strong result.

  • Voice cloning needs only about a minute of someone's voice

    Verified

    Commercial tools can clone a voice from about a minute of audio, and some research systems need only a few seconds.

  • Scammers use cloned voices to impersonate family members asking for money

    Verified

    The FTC warned about AI voice-clone 'family emergency' scams in March 2023, and the FBI has issued similar alerts.

  • Matt's Anchor Protocol phishing loss happened in 2020

    Needs Context

    Anchor Protocol launched on Terra in March 2021, so the incident was probably 2021 or later.

  • Harvard mapped one millimeter of the brain in about 10,000 slices

    Needs Context

    Harvard's Lichtman Lab and Google mapped one cubic millimeter of human cortex, cut into about 5,000 slices (Science, May 2024). The dataset was 1.4 petabytes.

  • The Psalms say we are little gods

    Verified

    Psalm 82:6: 'I said, You are gods, sons of the Most High, all of you.' Jesus quotes it in John 10:34.

  • An app lets you train an AI on your mom before she dies so you can FaceTime her afterward

    Verified

    2wai, promoted by Calum Worthy in November 2025, ran an ad showing exactly this and drew widespread backlash.

  • Marriage predates governments and universities

    Needs Context

    Some form of marriage appears in nearly every known society, and anthropologists generally treat it as older than states. Its exact origin can't be dated.

  • C. S. Lewis's The Abolition of Man deals with transhumanism

    Needs Context

    The 1943 book critiques 'Conditioners' who use technology and education to remake human nature. The word transhumanism came later, but the book is widely read as an early critique of it.

  • Elon Musk said we're already cyborgs because of our phones

    Verified

    Musk said at the 2016 Code Conference that 'we're already a cyborg' because of phones and computers.

  • Musk describes Grok as maximally truth-seeking

    Verified

    Musk has repeatedly said xAI aims for a 'maximally truth-seeking AI'.

  • Matt's brother's bank-scam loss was covered because his account was FDIC insured

    Incorrect

    FDIC insurance protects deposits only if the bank fails; it doesn't cover fraud or scams. Recoveries like this usually come from the bank's own fraud protections or Regulation E.
