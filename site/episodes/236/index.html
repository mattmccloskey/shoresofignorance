Fact Checks

  • Ernest Hemingway blew his brains out while rich and living in a mansion in Florida

    Incorrect

    Hemingway did die by a self-inflicted gunshot, on July 2, 1961, but it was at his house in Ketchum, Idaho, not in Florida. He had lived in Key West earlier and at the Finca Vigía in Cuba, and he was famous and financially successful, but the place of his death is Idaho.

  • Jeff Buckley's 'Hallelujah' is a cover of a Leonard Cohen song

    Verified

    Cohen wrote and first recorded 'Hallelujah' on Various Positions in 1984. Buckley's version, on Grace in 1994, is the cover that made the song famous for a much wider audience, which is the example Matt is using.

  • A cover album of Leonard Cohen songs came out in the early 2000s and included Aimee Mann, Rufus Wainwright, and maybe Fiona Apple

    Needs Context

    Matt is loose on the credits, and he says he is blanking on the artists. The closest match is the 2005 concert film and 2006 soundtrack Leonard Cohen: I'm Your Man, which does include Rufus Wainwright. Aimee Mann and Fiona Apple are not on that album; Mann recorded a Cohen cover years later, for other work. The 'early 2000s' dating and the Wainwright credit hold; the rest is a guess.

  • In No Country for Old Men, Tommy Lee Jones's character visits an old sheriff buddy in a wheelchair, pours a cup of coffee, recoils, asks how fresh it is, and is told that a fresh pot is made at least once a week

    Verified

    The scene is Bell's visit to his cousin Ellis, who is in a wheelchair. Ellis offers coffee, Bell takes a sip and pulls back, and Ellis says he generally makes a fresh pot every week even if some is left over. Matt has the tone and the joke right; 'at least once a week' is close to Ellis's actual line.

  • Bill Maher had dinner with Trump, Kid Rock facilitated it, Maher handed Trump a list of the names Trump had called him and Trump signed it, and Maher later said Trump was measured and kind and mostly wanted to know what Maher thought

    Verified

    The dinner was in April 2025. Kid Rock arranged it. On the April 11 Real Time monologue Maher called a 'book report,' he described bringing a printed list of Trump's insults, which Trump signed, and he said Trump was gracious and measured and spent much of the evening asking what Maher thought. As of this recording, April 16, 2025, that monologue was days old.

  • Kid Rock's real name is Bob, and he did a duet with Sheryl Crow

    Verified

    Kid Rock's given name is Robert James Ritchie, and he is often called Bob. The duet is 'Picture,' released in 2001, which is the early-aughts collaboration Matt remembers from MTV.

  • Solzhenitsyn said that the line between good and evil is drawn down the heart of every man

    Verified

    In The Gulag Archipelago Solzhenitsyn writes that 'the line dividing good and evil cuts through the heart of every human being.' Matt's 'drawn down the heart of every man' is the usual slight loosening of that sentence, and the attribution is right.

  • In The Lord of the Rings it takes Gandalf something like 50 years to act on the One Ring after he learns of its existence

    Needs Context

    In The Fellowship of the Ring, seventeen years pass after Bilbo's birthday party before Gandalf returns and confirms, in Frodo's fire, that the ring is the One Ring. The Council of Elrond then recounts the long inquiry with Aragorn, Gollum, and Saruman, which is the patience Matt is pointing to. 'Something like 50 years' overstates the interval after Gandalf first seriously takes up the question. If one counts from Bilbo's finding of the ring, the span is longer still, but that is not the wait the book dramatizes.

  • Leonard Cohen had died before this recording, though Matt says he does not even know if he is still alive

    Verified

    Cohen died on November 7, 2016. Matt is not claiming he is alive; he is admitting he does not know, which is the point of the riff about forming a relationship with someone you have never met. The underlying fact is that Cohen had already been gone for more than eight years.
