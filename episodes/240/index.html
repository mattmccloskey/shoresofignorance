Fact Checks

  • In the information age, we have declining birth rates, declining fertility, increased suicide rates in young people, and declining testosterone in men, and all of the information being available has made it harder to know what is true

    Needs Context

    The demographic and mental-health trends Matt lists were widely documented by 2025, including falling birth rates, a long decline in average testosterone, and a rise in youth suicide over the 2010s. The last clause is his judgment, not a measured finding, and 'all of the information' is doing a lot of work.

  • The placebo effect has been shown to be basically as strong as any other drug, at least maybe with psychological drugs

    Needs Context

    Matt already hedges. Placebo effects are real and can be substantial in pain, depression, and some other conditions, but they are not 'as strong as any other drug' as a general rule, and the claim depends entirely on the condition and the comparison.

  • G.K. Chesterton said, 'Reason itself is a matter of faith'

    Verified

    In Orthodoxy, in the chapter 'The Suicide of Thought,' Chesterton writes: 'Reason is itself a matter of faith. It is an act of faith to assert that our thoughts have any relation to reality at all.' Matt has the line and the sense right.

  • On a recent Joe Rogan episode, Jesse Michels, who worked at Peter Thiel's home office, and Rogan talked about UFOs, Bob Lazar, and the idea that AIs are going to become religions

    Verified

    Joe Rogan Experience #2331 with Jesse Michels came out on June 3, 2025, the day before this recording. Michels does describe work at Thiel's family office, and the conversation does go into UFOs, Lazar, and the cult-like or religious pull of particular AI systems. Matt is careful that he is not sure which of them said the religions line.

  • 90% of communication is nonverbal

    Incorrect

    This is the popular form of the Mehrabian myth. Albert Mehrabian's 1960s studies were about how people judged feelings from a single word when tone or facial expression conflicted with it, not about communication in general. The usual '93%' figure does not describe ordinary conversation, even if Matt is right that a lot of trust is read in the face and the body.

  • Chamath Palihapitiya said that an LLM is 'exquisite autocompletion'

    Verified

    That is Chamath's phrasing. He has described large language models as exquisite autocomplete: powerful next-token predictors, not oracles, which is the use Matt is making of the line.

  • How many women and children died before they figured out that washing your hands before assisting in a birth reduced infection rates? A million years worth of babies

    Needs Context

    The history is the Semmelweis story: mid-19th-century physicians resisted handwashing, and many mothers died of puerperal fever who need not have. 'A million years worth of babies' is rhetorical exaggeration, not a number.

  • A test just came out this week confirming that the male boxer from the Olympics has an XY karyotype and is male

    Needs Context

    In the first days of June 2025, a 2023 lab report on Olympic gold medalist Imane Khelif was published, describing an XY karyotype, and World Boxing had just said Khelif could not compete in its female category without a sex test. The timing of 'this week' is right. The rest is disputed: Khelif has always competed as a woman, the IOC said this was not a transgender case, and a DSD condition, if that is what the report points to, is not the same as 'he's male' in the sense Matt means.

  • Matt has a genetic condition called Dupuytren's syndrome, a buildup of fascia in the hand that threatens to prevent movement

    Verified

    That is a fair description of Dupuytren's contracture, also called Dupuytren's disease, in which fascia in the palm thickens and can draw the fingers down. He is reporting his own diagnosis.

  • In Gattaca, the protagonist lengthens his legs

    Verified

    Vincent does undergo a painful procedure to lengthen his legs so that his body will match the valid identity he is assuming. Matt is using the scene the way the film intends it.

  • The man who raced against Riley Gaines was a subprime, or not good enough, competitor in the men's league and then won in the women's field

    Needs Context

    Matt is thinking of Lia Thomas, who tied Gaines for fifth in the 2022 NCAA women's 200-yard freestyle. Thomas had been a mid-pack, not championship, swimmer in the men's ranks, which is the factual core of Michael's 'subprime' point, but 'subprime' is their epithet, and 'winning' overstates a fifth-place tie.
