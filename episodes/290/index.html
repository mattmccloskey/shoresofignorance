About This Episode

Matt and Michael mark eight years of the podcast with a question someone recently asked Michael. Does the devil exist? What follows is a winding conversation through evil, morality, materialism, and the logic of the cross. They explore why Western culture has lost touch with the concept of evil, how political contradictions reveal our moral confusion, and why the devil's greatest trick might be making himself disappear from the conversation entirely. Drawing on prison interviews, biblical texts, Solzhenitsyn, Scott Peck, and even Thanos, they land on a surprising conclusion. The devil is real, but he's not who we think he is. He's the spirit of self-justification, the voice that says "I am enough." And we all hear it.

Fact Checks

  • "Solzhenitsyn said the line between good and evil is drawn down the heart of every man"

    Verified

    From *The Gulag Archipelago*.

  • "Teresa of Avila said 'I want to want to love God'"

    Verified

    Matt corrects himself from a previous episode where he misattributed this to Mother Teresa.

  • "Gödel proved math cannot explain itself"

    Verified

    Gödel's incompleteness theorems (1931) demonstrate that any consistent formal system cannot prove its own consistency.

  • "Christ descended into hell"

    Biblical reference to 1 Peter 3:19 and Apostles' Creed

    Matt notes that "hell is outside of time" is an interpretation he heard recently, not established doctrine.

  • "Mormonism teaches Jesus and Satan are brothers"

    Verified

    This is a documented LDS teaching, though Matt and Michael note they may be oversimplifying.
