Fact Checks

  • Acetaminophen (Tylenol) is hard on the liver and especially dangerous with alcohol

    Verified

    Acetaminophen overdose is a leading cause of acute liver failure in the U.S.; product labels warn against combining with alcohol. Matt's liver caution matches standard medical guidance.

  • Bret Weinstein's work argued lab mice are a poor toxicity model because of telomere and breeding artifacts

    Verified

    Weinstein's research and later DarkHorse/Portal tellings argue captive mice were selected for traits that mute toxin effects. The Portal conversation Matt recalls (around Ep 19) popularized that critique; treat mechanistic details as contested among toxicologists.

  • Fever is partly an evolved immune response, so routinely suppressing mild fever can be counterproductive

    Needs Context

    Fever helps host defense, and many clinicians advise against reflex antipyretics for mild fever. Dangerous high fevers still warrant treatment; Matt's intuition is directionally right but not a blanket medical rule.

  • In Sep 2025 Trump and RFK Jr. publicly linked acetaminophen use in pregnancy to autism risk

    Verified

    The White House and HHS messaging in late Sep 2025 urged caution on acetaminophen in pregnancy and floated autism associations. The causal claim is heavily disputed by medical bodies; the announcement itself happened as Matt describes.

  • Some pregnant women filmed themselves taking Tylenol in protest of that announcement

    Needs Context

    Clips of defiant Tylenol-taking circulated on social media after the announcement. Scale is hard to measure; Matt's f-Trump framing matches the viral genre of those clips.

  • Charlie Kirk was assassinated in Sep 2025 and later given a large public memorial

    Verified

    Kirk was shot and killed on Sep 10, 2025, at a Utah campus event. A large memorial followed; attendance figures circulating at the time (including Matt's ~300,000) were media estimates, not an audited count.

  • Jason Calacanis said he watched dozens of hours of Charlie Kirk after the killing and came away impressed

    Needs Context

    On All-In and related appearances Calacanis described bingeing Kirk footage post-assassination and revising his prior caricature. Exact hour counts vary by retelling; Michael's 40–50 hours is in that ballpark.

  • Erica Kirk publicly forgave the alleged shooter at Charlie Kirk's memorial

    Verified

    In her memorial remarks Erica Kirk said she forgave Tyler Robinson. Contemporary coverage treated the forgiveness as the speech's defining moment, matching Matt's reaction.

  • Jimmy Kimmel was briefly taken off the air in Sep 2025 over comments about Charlie Kirk's killing, then reinstated

    Verified

    ABC suspended Jimmy Kimmel Live! after remarks about the Kirk shooting and returned the show days later amid affiliate and political pressure. Matt's canceled-then-reinstated summary is accurate.

  • The Bible says it is hard for a rich man to enter the kingdom of heaven, like a camel through the eye of a needle

    Verified

    Matthew 19:23–24 (and parallels) carry that teaching. Matt and Michael's insulation reading is a common pastoral gloss, not the only exeegesis.

  • Without faith it is impossible to please God; whoever comes to him must believe that he exists

    Verified

    Hebrews 11:6. Michael paraphrases it closely in the middle-path section.

  • Jesus on the cross said Father forgive them for they know not what they do

    Verified

    Luke 23:34. Michael ties Erica Kirk's forgiveness language to that line.

  • Kevin Hart stepped away from hosting the Oscars after old homophobic jokes resurfaced and he faced repeated apology demands

    Verified

    In 2018–2019 Hart withdrew as Oscars host after backlash over past tweets and refused an extended apology tour, matching Michael's example.
