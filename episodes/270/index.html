Fact Checks

  • Tolkien said he was as surprised by Strider as the hobbits were

    Verified

    Letter 163 (1955) to W. H. Auden: Strider sitting in the corner at the inn was a shock, and 'I had no more idea who he was than had Frodo.'

  • Sam Elliott plays Billy Bob Thornton's father in Landman season 2

    Verified

    Elliott joined season 2 (November 2025) as T. L. Norris, Tommy's father.

  • 'Win bigly' is a Scott Adams turn of phrase, and he 'used to be' around

    Needs Context

    'Bigly' comes from Donald Trump's 2016 debates (he was saying 'big league'). Adams used it in his 2017 book Win Bigly. Adams died on January 13, 2026, which explains Matt's 'he used to be, at least.'

  • McGilchrist: things become how you pay attention to them

    Verified

    A core claim of The Master and His Emissary (2009) is that the kind of attention we pay changes what we find in the world.

  • Jesus said 'you will do greater things than these'

    Verified

    John 14:12.

  • In A Wrinkle in Time, the children bounce balls in unison

    Verified

    On Camazotz, which is controlled by IT, children bounce balls and skip rope in perfect rhythm.

  • Elizabeth Gilbert's TED talk tells Tom Waits's story about a song arriving while he drove

    Verified

    Gilbert's 2009 talk tells it: Waits, on an LA freeway, tells the song 'Can't you see I'm driving?' and to go bother Leonard Cohen.

  • T. S. Eliot: old men should be explorers

    Verified

    East Coker (1940), from Four Quartets: 'Old men ought to be explorers.'

  • An actor asked Clint Eastwood how he stays young and Eastwood said 'don't let the old man in'

    Needs Context

    It was country singer Toby Keith, at a 2018 golf event, who asked about Eastwood's upcoming 88th birthday. Keith turned the line into a song for The Mule.

  • Gratitude changes your brain chemistry and helps with depression

    Needs Context

    Studies, including fMRI work and randomized trials of gratitude journaling, link gratitude practice to modest improvements in well-being and depressive symptoms. The effects are real but smaller than often claimed.

  • Salt on a steel plate bowed with a violin bow forms patterns

    Verified

    These are Chladni figures, first demonstrated by Ernst Chladni in 1787. Different contact points give different modes and patterns.

  • Smell brings back memory very powerfully

    Verified

    Odor-evoked memories tend to be more emotional and vivid, partly because the olfactory bulb connects directly to the amygdala and hippocampus.

  • Willie Nelson's guitar has a name

    Verified

    Trigger, a Martin N-20 classical guitar he has played since 1969.

  • In JJK, the stronger domain wins and pulls opponents into its environment

    Verified

    Domain Expansion in Jujutsu Kaisen; when two domains clash, the stronger one overpowers the other.
