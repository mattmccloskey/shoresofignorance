About This Episode

Matt and Michael open with a meditation on purpose. Why do they do this podcast? No agenda, no growth targets, no monetization strategy. Just two friends showing up every Wednesday for six years. Matt connects this to Goodhart's Law. When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure. Michael shares how the same principle applies to running Medici. You can get so lost in cost of goods and ticket counts that you forget why you started.

Fact Checks

  • Goodhart's Law

    Correctly attributed

    The original formulation is by economist Charles Goodhart: "Any observed statistical regularity will tend to collapse once pressure is placed upon it for control purposes." The popular phrasing "when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure" is a later simplification by Marilyn Strathern.

  • Toka Group

    Real company

    Founded in Israel in 2017, later relocated to Massachusetts. They do claim capabilities for remotely accessing and manipulating security camera systems. Their technology has been discussed on Dark Horse and other podcasts.

  • Anthropic watermarking

    Anthropic has publicly discussed watermarking techniques for LLM output

    The technical description Matt gave (token-level mathematical signatures embedded in output) is consistent with published research on LLM watermarking.

  • Ring footage recovery

    In 2024, it was revealed that Ring retained footage even from cameras that users believed were destroyed or had deleted footage from

    This was widely reported.

  • Flock Safety cameras

    Real company deploying automated license plate readers (ALPRs) across the US

    Privacy concerns have been raised by the ACLU and EFF.

  • Canadian trucker convoy debanking

    During the 2022 Freedom Convoy protests, the Canadian government invoked the Emergencies Act, which led to freezing of bank accounts of protesters and donors without court orders.
