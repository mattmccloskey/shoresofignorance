About This Episode

Everything has value, whether you measure it in currency or material goods. Dave Keisel, an engineer turned seasoned stock market trader, joins us to share his unique perspective on technology and money. In today’s episode, Matt, Michael, and Dave explore the concept of assigning value, and its moral implications.
