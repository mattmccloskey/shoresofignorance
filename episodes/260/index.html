About This Episode

The internet often makes us feel like non-player characters in the world. We explore this phenomenon and what correctives might look like. Things as simple as a compliment or an acknowledgment can transform an NPC into a main character, like the black and white changes to color in the Wizard of Oz.

Fact Checks

  • A Diary of a CEO guest said he could start a business with $500, using about $100 to learn AI tools and the rest on Meta ads, then pepper small businesses with questions and build AI solutions

    Verified

    The clip is from Chris Koerner's episode of The Diary of a CEO, released December 8, 2025, two days before this recording. Koerner said he would spend about $100 learning no-code AI tools and building a site, put the rest into local Meta ads, get business owners on discovery calls and pepper them with questions, then charge $500 to $5,000 to implement a solution plus about 20 percent a year to maintain it.

  • Nine out of ten businesses fail

    Needs Context

    As of December 2025 the nine in ten figure is usually cited for venture-backed startups, not businesses in general. Bureau of Labor Statistics data show about 20 percent of new private-sector establishments close within a year, and averaged over 1994 to 2022 about 49 percent survived five years and about 34 percent survived ten. Most businesses do fail eventually, but not nine in ten.

  • The Uchi founder said sushi chefs spend their first three years only making rice

    Needs Context

    Uchi was founded in Austin by Tyson Cole, who has said it took him about ten years of training to become a sushi chef. Traditional Japanese sushi apprenticeships do often keep trainees on rice and prep for years before they handle fish, but there is no fixed three-year rule and the advice Matt heard secondhand could not be confirmed.

  • Accenture employs half a million people

    Needs Context

    Accenture passed about half a million employees around 2020, which fits Matt's time there. As of the end of fiscal 2025 (August 31, 2025) it reported about 779,000 employees.

  • Luck favors the prepared

    Verified

    The line comes from Louis Pasteur, who said in an 1854 lecture that in the fields of observation chance favors only the prepared mind.

  • Abraham Lincoln was once so depressed that his family brought him food but would not let him have a knife

    Needs Context

    In early 1841, after breaking off his engagement to Mary Todd, Lincoln fell into a suicidal depression. His friend Joshua Speed later recalled that they had to remove razors from his room and take away all knives. It was friends, not family, who kept watch, and Lincoln had a similar crisis in 1835.

  • T.S. Eliot said old men should be explorers

    Verified

    From East Coker (1940), the second of the Four Quartets: 'Old men ought to be explorers.'

  • A man who turned 55 posted 55 things on Twitter, including that if you have a compliment for someone you should give it

    Verified

    Kevin Dahlstrom posted 'Today I turn 55' on X on November 24, 2025, with 55 habits. Number 4 reads 'If you have a positive thought about someone, don't keep it to yourself, share it immediately.' The thread drew millions of views.

  • The Metro coffee shop was in the space that is now Medici on Guadalupe

    Verified

    Metro Espresso Bar was at 2222 Guadalupe Street on the Drag. The space reopened as Caffe Medici in 2008 and is now Medici Roasting's Guadalupe cafe.

  • The Witcher's main character left because the show was moving in a woke direction

    Needs Context

    Henry Cavill announced in October 2022 that he was leaving the role of Geralt, and Liam Hemsworth took over for season 4, which premiered October 30, 2025. Cavill did not give a reason beyond the announcement, and reports that he left over the show's departures from the books were never confirmed by him or Netflix, so the woke explanation is speculation.

  • In The Wizard of Oz, the world turns from black and white to color

    Verified

    The 1939 film shows Kansas in sepia tones and switches to Technicolor when Dorothy opens the door into Oz.
