Fact Checks

  • Elon has always voted left, up until this election

    Needs Context

    Musk has said he voted for Democratic presidential candidates in the prior three presidential elections. In June 2022, though, he also said a vote for Mayra Flores in a Texas special election was the first time he had ever voted Republican, so the switch was already under way before the 2024 race. Michael is right about the long Democratic pattern and a bit early on 'this election' as the first break.

  • In 1984 there are three superpower states, Oceania, East Asia, and Eurasia, always at war, and when the enemy changes they rewrite history and say they have always been at war with the new one; there is a literal memory hole for outdated information

    Verified

    That is the plot of Orwell's Nineteen Eighty-Four. The familiar slogan is 'we have always been at war with Eastasia,' and the Ministry of Truth's memory hole is where inconvenient records go.

  • It was the Democrats, the ACLU, that fought for the right of the Nazis to march in New York

    Needs Context

    The landmark fight is the ACLU's 1977-78 defense of the National Socialist Party of America's right to march in Skokie, Illinois, not New York. Matt even hedges with 'what, New York?' He is pointing at that tradition and has the city wrong.

  • Annie Duke, the poker player, wrote Thinking in Bets, about putting a percentage on what you believe

    Verified

    Thinking in Bets (2018) is Duke's book on uncertainty and probabilistic thinking. Michael briefly calls it 'Thinking in Betsy,' and Matt supplies the title.

  • They're eating the cats, they're eating the dogs, and there were actual cases of Haitians doing that

    Needs Context

    Matt treats the September 2024 debate line as a hyperbolic way of forcing a subject into view and says he does not really know whether it happened. Springfield, Ohio, officials and police said they had no credible evidence that Haitian immigrants were stealing or eating pets; viral 'evidence' pointed to unrelated cases in other cities. Michael is right that isolated animal-cruelty cases exist, and wrong to hang them on Springfield's Haitian community as if the degree were merely unknown.

  • The point of the Sermon on the Mount is, you have heard it said that you should not do X, Y, Z, but I tell you that you are all guilty of that whether you have done it or not

    Verified

    In Matthew 5, Jesus takes the received commandments against murder and adultery and extends them to anger and lust in the heart. Matt's paraphrase is faithful, including the sense that the standard is not met by a clean external record.

  • Israel means we who wrestle with God

    Verified

    In Genesis 32:28, Jacob is renamed Israel, commonly explained as 'he struggles with God' or 'God strives.' Matt is recalling a point from the previous episode, and the etymology holds.

  • RFK Jr. said in a confirmation hearing that there are no Democrat or Republican children

    Verified

    At his Senate Finance Committee confirmation hearing on January 29, 2025, the same day this episode is dated, Robert F. Kennedy Jr. said, 'We have to remember that there are no Democrat or Republican children.' That is the line Matt asks Michael to pause on.
