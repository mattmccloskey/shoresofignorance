About This Episode

Although he was a son, he learned obedience through what he suffered. Michael has been sitting with that Hebrews line, and with the other voice that says I don't want to suffer anymore, I want to buy the boat and fish.

Matt wonders if suffering is closer to the root of things than we want, and if you learn obedience by looking for it. We talk about purpose you're still discovering, words that have to be your own, and repentance that only works if someone will reach for who you are becoming.

Fact Checks

  • Kill Tony has been around for 13 years

    Verified

    Matt (1:35) said he "didn't know Kill Tony's been around for 13 years." The show premiered June 3, 2013, so at the recording date it was about 13 years and 5 months old.

  • The Hebrews 5:7-10 reading matches the text

    Verified

    Michael (4:11) reads Hebrews 5:7-8 in wording that matches the ESV: "In the days of his flesh, Jesus offered up prayers and supplications with loud cries and tears to him who was able to save him from death, and he was heard because of his reverence. Although he was a son, he learned obedience through what he suffered." Matt later uses the NIV's "petitions," "fervent cries," and "reverent submission" (11:17, 30:25), and at 78:47 he continues through 5:9-10 almost exactly: made perfect, he became the source of eternal salvation for all who obey him, designated by God a high priest in the order of Melchizedek.

  • Jesus said he had done all the Father gave him to do in John 15 or 16

    Needs Context

    Michael (13:19) said, "I think it's in John 15 or 16 or something like that... God, I did everything that you gave me to do." The line is John 17:4, in the High Priestly Prayer: "I glorified you on earth, having accomplished the work that you gave me to do."

  • The paralytic Jesus healed was at the well in John 4

    Incorrect

    Michael (13:19) said "the guy who was at the well in, you know, John 4, and he wasn't the only person. Paralytic person at that well, there was a lot of people that were sick... he just healed that one person," then correctly described the Samaritan woman at the well. Those are two different scenes. John 4 is the Samaritan woman. The man who had been an invalid for 38 years is at the pool of Bethesda in John 5, where Jesus heals him and does not heal everyone present.

  • The author of Hebrews is Paul

    Needs Context

    Matt (17:21) said he understands "the author of Hebrews to possibly be Paul." Michael answered, "Yeah, I'll just say Peter, but I think it is attributed to Paul." The letter is anonymous. It was traditionally attributed to Paul, especially in the East, but that has been doubted since antiquity, and modern scholarship generally rejects Pauline authorship on grounds of style and theology. It is not attributed to Peter.

  • "You will always have the poor" and "you will not always have me"

    Verified

    Michael (13:19) said Jesus talks about always having the poor, and Matt (15:30) added, "But you, you will only have me for a little while longer. Something like that." Matthew 26:11, Mark 14:7, and John 12:8 all have the pairing: the poor you always have with you, but you will not always have me.

  • Jesus' family came to take him, thinking he was out of his mind

    Verified

    Michael (25:47) said that at one point Jesus' people "go to get him because they think he's a crazy person," and when Matt asked which story, he placed it in Mark: "his mother and his brothers go to this house and they're like, coming to get him." Mark 3:21 says his family went to seize him, for people were saying, "He is out of his mind." Mark 3:31-35 then has his mother and brothers standing outside, and Jesus pointing to those who do the will of God as his family.

  • Indoor smoking is still allowed in Las Vegas casinos

    Verified

    Matt (3:17) said he "hadn't been in a building where you could smoke inside in a very long time," after spending three days at a Vegas hotel. Nevada's Clean Indoor Air Act still allows smoking on casino gaming floors where minors are prohibited. It is banned in restaurants and many other indoor areas, and Park MGM is a notable smoke-free exception on the Strip, but indoor smoking in casinos remains legal and common.

  • A traditional cappuccino is 6 to 8 ounces

    Needs Context

    Michael (22:55) said "a traditional cappuccinos, like 6 to 8 ounces." A classic Italian cappuccino is typically about 5 to 6 ounces (150-180 ml). Eight ounces is a common larger cafe size, not the traditional one. His contrast with a 20-ounce drink still holds.

  • C.S. Lewis said no one knows how bad he is till he has tried to be good

    Verified

    Matt (62:22) said, "Lewis says. CS Lewis says the only people who have tried really hard to be good know very much about badness at all... It isn't until you try to become good that you realize how bad you are." In Mere Christianity, Book III, Chapter 11, Lewis writes, "No man knows how bad he is till he has tried very hard to be good," and "That is why bad people, in one sense, know very little about badness." Matt's wording is a close paraphrase.

  • Kevin Hart was dropped as Emmys host over a 2006 slur

    Incorrect

    Matt (73:28) said, "Kevin Hart's gonna host the Emmys. Then they found out he said faggot back in 2006, or whatever it was that he said. And then he apologizes, and they're like, this is more evidence that you're a piece of shit." He did apologize, and the online pile-on is the part of Matt's point that stands.

  • Melchizedek was a priest from another tribe

    Needs Context

    Michael (79:48) said Melchizedek "was kind of like another tribe that Abraham tithed to," "kind of accepted that Melzedek was also like, kind of a priest of God and of another tribe," and "through Melzedek, Abraham offered tithes to God. And so that's sort of like Jesus is another Melzedek." Genesis 14:18-20 does have Abraham giving a tenth to Melchizedek, king of Salem and priest of God Most High. "Another tribe" is loose: this is before Israel's tribes exist. Hebrews 7 uses the scene to argue that Jesus is a priest in Melchizedek's order, not that Jesus is "another Melchizedek."

  • Teresa of Avila prayed "I want to want to love you"

    Needs Context

    Matt (84:35) cited "that Teresa quote, God, I do not love you. I don't even want to love you, but I want to want to love you." The prayer is widely attributed to St. Teresa of Avila, usually in the form "O God, I do not love you. I do not want to love you. But I want to want to love you." A passage in her own writings was not located; treat it as a traditional attribution rather than a confirmed quotation.

  • Peter said "to whom shall we go" after the feeding of the 5,000

    Verified

    Matt (91:12) asked what Peter says "after he feeds the 5,000 and a bunch of people who are following Jesus leave," and Michael answered, "Where else shall we go? You have the words to eternal life?" In John 6, the feeding is followed by the Bread of Life discourse; many disciples turn back, and Peter says, "Lord, to whom shall we go? You have the words of eternal life."

  • The Oracle told Socrates he was wise because he did not think he was wise

    Needs Context

    Matt (83:51) said "what did the Oracle of Delphi told Socrates that he was wise because he did not think he was wise." In Plato's Apology, the oracle says no one is wiser than Socrates. Socrates investigates this, and concludes that he is wise only in not thinking he knows what he does not know. The popular wording collapses the oracle's answer and Socrates' interpretation.

  • Queen Elizabeth "just died recently," and The Crown is a Netflix special

    Needs Context

    Matt (10:48) asked if Queen Elizabeth was "the one that just died recently," and Michael (6:13) called The Crown "that Netflix special about the Queen Elizabeth," quoting something like "the crown is here to serve." Elizabeth II died on September 8, 2022, a little over four years before this recording. The Crown is a multi-season Netflix series, not a special. Duty and service are a major theme of the show and of Elizabeth's public ethos; an exact "the crown is here to serve" line was not located.
